Sirius AI GRC

Governance is not a plugin. It is the runtime foundation.

Sirius AI GRC is the governance layer of the platform: policy management, runtime checkpoints, approval governance, agent bill of materials, evidence, audit, standards mapping, and Control Tower visibility.

Runtime policy engine

Policies are checked where AI actually runs: prompts, tools, skills, agents, flows, integrations, and deployment transitions.

Approval governance

Human approvals are part of execution, not email afterthoughts. Pause, assign, approve, resume, and prove the decision path.

Agent bill of materials

ABOM makes every agent explainable by listing its prompts, models, tools, data bindings, guardrails, and integrations.

Evidence by default

Execution events, artifacts, lineage, replay details, and control outcomes become audit evidence automatically.

Standards mapping

Map controls to industry and regulatory frameworks without rebuilding the same governance inventory repeatedly.

Layman AI control

Business owners can understand risk, approve actions, and inspect outcomes without becoming AI platform engineers.
